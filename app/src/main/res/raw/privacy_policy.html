Privacy Policy

JTR — Just To Remember  ·  Last updated: August 2026

Summary: JTR is local-first: your personal contact data never leaves your device. The only network use is for place features — geocoding (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) and map tiles (OpenFreeMap) — which send a city name/coordinates and your IP address, never your contact data. No account, no ads, no analytics.

1Data collected

The app stores the following information solely for managing your contacts:

  • First name, last name, gender
  • Birthday and other important dates
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Job title, company and department
  • City of residence and associated GPS coordinates
  • Links to social network profiles
  • Relationships between your contacts
  • Categories and folders you create
  • Origin, interests, notes and note sections
  • Profile photo and category cover images (stored locally)

2Data storage

✓ 100% local — All your data is stored exclusively on your device via an SQLite database (Room). No remote server, no account and no advertising.

✓ Permanent deletion — Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data.

3Location & proximity reminders

The Proximity Reminder feature is optional and off by default. If you explicitly enable it, JTR accesses your location — including in the background (even when the app is closed) — to:

  • Calculate the distance between your position and your contacts' cities (on-device)
  • Trigger a local notification when you pass near a contact for which you turned the reminder on (at most one alert per contact every 48 h)

✓ Your location is processed only on the device and is never transmitted to the publisher or any third party. You can disable the feature at any time (Settings → Proximity Reminders); it resets automatically if you revoke the location permission.

Location detection and geofencing rely on Google Play services, which may use their own location mechanisms (an Android/Google system behavior), independently of JTR.

4Photo access

Photo access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13+, or partial "selected photos" access on Android 14+) is requested only when you choose a profile or cover image:

  • The in-app picker reads your gallery locally to display your albums and photos
  • The image you select is cropped and stored on your device only

✓ No photo or gallery data is uploaded or shared. You can grant access to all photos, only selected ones, or none.

5Third-party services

Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)

When you search or select a city (or tap a point on the map), the city name or coordinates are sent to nominatim.openstreetmap.org for geocoding. Transmitted: the search term and your IP address — no contact data, no identifier, no account.

Policy: osmfoundation.org

OpenFreeMap

Displaying the map downloads map tiles from tiles.openfreemap.org (the displayed area and your IP address). No contact data is sent.

Policy: openfreemap.org

✓ No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK is integrated. The only Google library is Play Services Location, used for the optional proximity feature.

6Notifications

JTR notifications are entirely local and do not transit through any server:

  • Birthday & important-date reminders — scheduled and triggered locally, with a configurable lead time per date (exact on-device alarms)
  • Proximity reminders — triggered locally via WorkManager or Geofencing

✓ On Android 13+, the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission is requested only to display these local reminders. You can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

7App lock (optional)

JTR offers an optional local lock — an unlock pattern with a recovery code, plus optional biometric unlock:

✓ Only salted fingerprints (hashes) of your pattern and recovery code are kept, encrypted on your device (EncryptedSharedPreferences via the Android Keystore). The pattern and the code themselves are never stored in clear text and never transmitted.

✓ No biometric data is read or stored by the app — biometric authentication is handled entirely by the Android system.

8Import from your phone (optional)

If you choose to import contacts, JTR asks for permission to read your device's contact list (READ_CONTACTS) and copies only the entries you select.

✓ Imported information is stored locally in JTR, exactly like the data you enter yourself, and is never sent anywhere. You can decline this permission, or revoke it at any time in Android settings.

9Sharing & backup (only when you act)

You can share a contact's card as text, an image or a PDF, and export all your data to a .jtr backup file.

✓ In every case the information goes only to the app or destination you choose, and only when you trigger the action. JTR never shares, uploads or synchronises your data on its own.

10Your rights

  • Access and modification — all your data is visible and editable in the app
  • Deletion — any contact or category can be deleted (30-day trash)
  • Permanent deletion — uninstalling the app erases all data
  • Permission control — location and notifications can be revoked at any time in Android settings

11Minors

JTR does not knowingly collect data from persons under 13 years of age and does not target this audience.
