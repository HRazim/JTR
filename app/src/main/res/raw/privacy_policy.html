Privacy Policy

JTR — Just To Remember  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Summary: JTR is a 100% local app. Your personal data never leaves your device, except for city geocoding (Nominatim).

1Data collected

The app stores the following information solely for managing your contacts:

  • First name, last name, gender
  • Birthday
  • City of residence and associated GPS coordinates
  • Links to social network profiles
  • Origin, interests and personal notes
  • Profile photo (stored locally)

2Data storage

✓ 100% local — All your data is stored exclusively on your device via an SQLite database (Room). No remote server, no account and no advertising.

✓ Permanent deletion — Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data.

3Location usage

Location permission is requested only for the Proximity Reminder feature:

  • Calculating the distance between your position and your contacts' cities (Haversine formula)
  • Triggering a local notification when you are near a contact you haven't reached out to in a while

✓ No location data is transmitted to third parties or stored outside your device.

You can disable this feature at any time: Settings → Proximity Reminders.

4Photo access

Photo access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13+, or partial "selected photos" access on Android 14+) is requested only when you choose a profile or cover image:

  • The in-app picker reads your gallery locally to display your albums and photos
  • The image you select is cropped and stored on your device only

✓ No photo or gallery data is uploaded or shared. You can grant access to all photos, only selected ones, or none.

5Third-party services

Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)

When searching or selecting a city, the city name is sent to the Nominatim API for geocoding. No personal data is transmitted.

Usage policy: nominatim.org

✓ No other third-party services (analytics, advertising, crash reporting) are integrated.

6Notifications

JTR notifications are entirely local and do not transit through any server:

  • Birthday & important-date reminders — scheduled and triggered locally, with a configurable lead time per date (exact on-device alarms)
  • Proximity reminders — triggered locally via WorkManager or Geofencing

✓ On Android 13+, the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission is requested only to display these local reminders. You can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

7App lock (optional)

JTR offers an optional local lock — an unlock pattern with a recovery code, plus optional biometric unlock:

✓ Only salted fingerprints (hashes) of your pattern and recovery code are kept, encrypted on your device (EncryptedSharedPreferences via the Android Keystore). The pattern and the code themselves are never stored in clear text and never transmitted.

✓ No biometric data is read or stored by the app — biometric authentication is handled entirely by the Android system.

8Your rights

  • Access and modification — all your data is visible and editable in the app
  • Deletion — any contact or category can be deleted (30-day trash)
  • Permanent deletion — uninstalling the app erases all data
  • Permission control — location and notifications can be revoked at any time in Android settings

9Minors

JTR does not knowingly collect data from persons under 13 years of age and does not target this audience.
